Shared group mission

Rebuild the world before the continents drifted apart.

Your group has received fragments of an ancient world. Work together in this shared room to arrange the continents and place the fossil and climate clues. Look for coastlines that seem to fit and notice where the same kinds of ancient organisms or climate traces appear. Observe, test, and discuss your ideas. You do not need to know the scientific terms yet.

🔎 Investigation briefing

One person moving a puzzle piece changes the shared board for everyone. Discuss your group's decisions in your breakout room. Each learner answers the guide questions individually after the simulation.
